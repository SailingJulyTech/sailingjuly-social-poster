SailingJuly Social Auto-Poster

Last updated: 2026-09-04

SailingJuly Social Auto-Poster is a personal automation tool that publishes the SailingJuly creator's own pre-produced video content to the SailingJuly Facebook Page, Instagram account, and TikTok account on a schedule. It is a small, single-user script, not a public app or service offered to other users — it has no sign-up and no audience-facing component.

What it does

A scheduled job reads a queue of already-finished SailingJuly videos and posts each one to the connected accounts at its scheduled time, using each platform's official posting API.
